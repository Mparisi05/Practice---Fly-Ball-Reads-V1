🧠 Runner on 2nd — Fly Ball Reads

Start with your lead, then take your secondary as the pitch comes. When the ball is hit in the air, read the situation:

🔵 Deep fly — outfielder going back

One-Third Way Rule: Get roughly ⅓ of the way to third. You should be 3 hard steps from getting back to 2nd.

If the outfielder is drifting back and the ball gets over their head → you score easily.

If they catch it going backwards → 3 steps back, touch the bag, hold.

🟠 Medium-deep fly — outfielder camped under it

If the fielder is settled and camped under a ball that's deep enough for you to tag and beat the throw to 3rd → tag immediately.

💡 Key read: Is the throw from where the fielder catches it far enough that I can beat it to 3rd? If yes — tag up on contact.
🟢 Short fly — near the infield

Ball is hit shallow enough that you cannot tag and make it to 3rd.

Get as far off the bag as possible toward 3rd — if it drops, you score. If it's caught, you need to get back before the throw reaches 2nd.

💡 Aggressive off the base, but not so far you can't get back on a catch-and-throw to 2B.
🧠 Runner on 1st — Fly Ball Reads

Start with your lead, then take your secondary. On a fly ball to right field:

🟣 Key rule: Rarely tag from 1st

The throw from right field to 2nd base is short. Tagging up from 1st on a fly ball to RF almost always gets you thrown out.

💡 Instead: Get as far off 1st as you can while the ball is in the air — but always be able to get back if the fielder catches it and throws to 1st.
🟢 If the ball drops

Because you've extended your lead aggressively, you're already halfway to 2nd → advance easily.

🔵 If it's caught

Retreat back to 1st safely. The outfielder's throw will likely go to 2nd or a cutoff — not back to 1st unless you're way too far off.

Mindset: Be aggressive but smart. Your job is to gain free distance — not to tag.
